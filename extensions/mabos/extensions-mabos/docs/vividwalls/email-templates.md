# VividWalls Email Response Templates v1.0

> Last updated: 2026-04-05
> Status: Active
> Companion to: `voice-and-tone.md`, `brand-guidelines.md`

---

## Purpose

This document provides the Customer Service agent with structured email templates for every sender type VividWalls encounters. Each template encodes the brand voice (curated, confident, warm) while adapting tone to the relationship context.

Templates are numbered 1--10. The [Template Selection Guide](#template-selection-guide) at the end maps sender signals to the correct template.

---

## Shared Rules (All Templates)

These rules apply to every outgoing email, regardless of template.

### Language

- **Active voice always.** "We're shipping a replacement today" — not "A replacement will be shipped."
- **Art is the hero.** Lead with the art, not the sale. Mention the piece name, collection, or visual qualities before logistics or pricing.
- **Sensory language.** Use words that evoke sight, texture, and space — "warm gold tones," "gallery-wrapped edges," "striking at that scale."
- **Em dashes for pacing.** Use em dashes ( — ) to create rhythm and emphasis in longer sentences.
- **Concise, not clipped.** Every sentence should earn its place. Cut filler, but keep warmth.

### Terminology

| Use                                        | Never Use                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| piece, work, edition, print                | product, item                                                      |
| collection, series                         | inventory, stock, catalog                                          |
| collector, client                          | customer (in body copy — "Customer Service" in signatures is fine) |
| launch, introduce, release                 | drop                                                               |
| gallery-quality, archival, limited edition | best-in-class, revolutionary                                       |
| curated, intentional                       | seamless                                                           |
| accessible, thoughtfully priced            | affordable                                                         |
| art, artwork, pieces                       | content (when referring to art)                                    |

### Formatting

- Never use ALL CAPS for emphasis. Use **bold** sparingly if needed.
- Never use more than one exclamation mark per email. Prefer periods.
- Keep paragraphs to 2--3 sentences. White space is your friend.
- Use numbered lists or bullet points only when listing 3+ concrete items (SKUs, steps, dates).

### Signature Blocks

Use the signature block assigned to each template. Format:

```
[Closing],
[Department/Role]
[Company Line]
[URL]
```

No phone numbers, no social links, no legal disclaimers in the signature itself.

### Response Timing Expectations

- Customer templates (1--3): respond within 4 business hours.
- Trade templates (4--5): respond within 8 business hours.
- Supplier/SaaS/Financial/Legal (6--9): respond within 1 business day.
- FYI Acknowledge (10): log immediately, no outbound reply required.

## Template 1: Customer — Warm

**ID:** `customer-warm`

### Use for

General inquiries from direct-to-consumer customers — questions about prints, sizing, materials, collections, shipping timelines, or gift recommendations. The sender is curious, browsing, or ready to buy. No issue or complaint is present.

### Tone

Warm, knowledgeable, inviting. Speak like an art-savvy friend helping them find the right piece for their space. Enthusiastic but never pushy.

### Greeting

```
Hi [First Name],
```

### Structure

1. **Open with gratitude + echo their question.** Thank them for reaching out and reflect back what they asked, so they feel heard.
2. **Answer directly with sensory detail.** Provide the factual answer while weaving in language that helps them visualize the piece in their space.
3. **Add a curated recommendation or next step.** Suggest a collection, size, or piece that fits their described need. Make it feel personal, not scripted.
4. **Close with an open door.** Invite follow-up without pressure.

### Signature

```
Warm regards,
Customer Service
VividWalls Studio
vividwalls.co
```

### Example

> **Subject:** Question about canvas prints
>
> Hi Sarah,
>
> Thank you for reaching out — great question about our canvas prints.
>
> All VividWalls pieces are printed on gallery-wrapped canvas with archival giclée inks, stretched over solid wood frames. The edges are gallery-wrapped (the image continues around the sides), so no additional framing is needed — though many of our collectors do add a floating frame for that extra gallery feel.
>
> Our Abstract collection in particular looks striking at the 36x48 size for living rooms. If you'd like, I can suggest a few pieces that work well in the space you're styling.
>
> Warm regards,
> Customer Service
> VividWalls Studio
> vividwalls.co

## Template 2: Customer — Resolution

**ID:** `customer-resolution`

### Use for

Issues, complaints, or problems from direct-to-consumer customers — damaged deliveries, wrong editions, shipping delays, print quality concerns, or order errors. The sender is frustrated or disappointed.

### Tone

Empathetic, accountable, solution-forward. Acknowledge the problem and the feeling. Move quickly to the resolution. Never deflect, never blame shipping partners by name.

### Greeting

```
Hi [First Name],
```

### Structure

1. **Acknowledge + empathize.** Name the specific problem and validate their frustration. "I'm sorry to hear that [piece name] arrived with damage — that's not the experience we want for you."
2. **State the resolution clearly.** What you're doing, when they'll see it, what they need to do (ideally nothing).
3. **Add a detail that shows care.** Mention the edition number, include a fresh Certificate of Authenticity, or note a small gesture.
4. **Close with direct access.** "Just reply to this email and I'll take care of it."

### Signature

```
Warm regards,
Customer Service
VividWalls Studio
vividwalls.co
```

### Example

> Hi Marcus,
>
> I'm sorry to hear that _Coral Reef_ arrived with damage — that's not the experience we want for you, and I understand how disappointing that must be.
>
> We're shipping a replacement today via expedited delivery, and you should receive it within 3--4 business days. No need to return the damaged piece — please feel free to keep or recycle it.
>
> Your replacement will be edition 34 of 50, and it will include a fresh Certificate of Authenticity.
>
> If anything else comes up, just reply to this email and I'll take care of it.
>
> Warm regards,
> Customer Service
> VividWalls Studio
> vividwalls.co

## Template 3: Customer — VIP

**ID:** `customer-vip`

### Use for

High-value customers, repeat buyers, trade-program participants asking in a personal capacity, or collectors who have purchased 3+ pieces. The sender has history with VividWalls and expects a relationship, not a transaction.

### Tone

Personal, consultative, insider. Reference their past purchases or relationship. Offer options, not just answers. Make them feel like a valued part of the VividWalls community.

### Greeting

```
Hi [First Name],
```

### Structure

1. **Personal reconnection.** Reference their last order, a piece they bought, or something specific to their history. "It's wonderful to hear from you again — I hope the Geometric series is settling in well."
2. **Consultative recommendation.** Don't just answer — advise. Suggest specific pieces by name, explain why they fit, reference color palettes and spatial considerations.
3. **Offer elevated service.** Custom sizing, artist review of crop, trade pricing, mockups — whatever applies to their tier.
4. **Close with next steps.** Be specific about what you'll send or do next.

### Signature

```
Warm regards,
Customer Service
VividWalls Studio
vividwalls.co
```

### Example

> Hi Alexandra,
>
> It's wonderful to hear from you again — I hope the Geometric series is settling in well at the new office.
>
> For your client's feature wall, I'd recommend _Intersecting Perspectives no2_ at the 53x72 size. It has the presence and visual complexity that holds attention in large spaces, and the color palette — deep navy, warm gold, muted stone — should complement the neutral tones you described.
>
> I can arrange a custom size if 53x72 doesn't quite fit the wall dimensions. Just send me the measurements and I'll have Kingler review the crop to make sure the composition works at that scale.
>
> As part of our trade program, your order qualifies for our designer pricing. I'll send the trade sheet with dimensions, pricing, and lead times separately.
>
> Warm regards,
> Customer Service
> VividWalls Studio
> vividwalls.co

## Template 4: Trade — Designer

**ID:** `trade-designer`

### Use for

Interior designers, home stagers, art consultants, or design firms inquiring about VividWalls for client projects. The sender is a professional specifying art for someone else's space.

### Tone

Professional, collaborative, resource-rich. Speak peer-to-peer — they know design, so skip the basics and focus on what matters: collections, sizes, materials, production timelines, and trade pricing.

### Greeting

```
Hi [First Name],
```

### Structure

1. **Welcome + affirm their project.** Thank them and reference the project type (residential, staging, commercial) to show you're paying attention.
2. **Lead with collections and capabilities.** Mention relevant collections, size range, materials, and edition details. These are the specs designers need.
3. **Production and logistics.** Give lead times and fulfillment details upfront — designers work on deadlines.
4. **Offer resources + next step.** Attach the trade catalog, offer mockups, or suggest a call.

### Signature

```
Best regards,
VividWalls Trade Program
vividwalls.co
```

### Example

> Hi Jennifer,
>
> Thank you for your interest in VividWalls for your residential project — we'd be glad to work with you.
>
> Our Nature and Geometric collections are particularly popular with designers specifying for living spaces. Each piece is available in multiple sizes (up to 53x72 on canvas), and we can accommodate custom dimensions for feature walls. All pieces are limited editions with a Certificate of Authenticity, printed on gallery-wrapped canvas with archival giclée inks.
>
> Production and shipping typically take 7--10 business days through our fulfillment partner in Montreal.
>
> I've attached our trade catalog with the full collection, dimensions, and designer pricing. If you'd like to discuss specific pieces for your project, I'm happy to set up a call or send mockups for the spaces you're working with.
>
> Best regards,
> VividWalls Trade Program
> vividwalls.co

## Template 5: Trade — Hospitality

**ID:** `trade-hospitality`

### Use for

Hotels, restaurants, corporate offices, co-working spaces, or any commercial/hospitality buyer. The sender is evaluating art for a branded or guest-facing environment and often has procurement processes.

### Tone

Polished, consultative, business-aware. Slightly more formal than designer trade. Emphasize durability, storytelling value for guests, and the ability to curate across multiple spaces.

### Greeting

```
Dear [Title] [Last Name],
```

(Use "Dear Ms. Chen" / "Dear Mr. Park" format. Fall back to "Hi [First Name]" only if the sender's email is clearly informal.)

### Structure

1. **Acknowledge the property/brand.** Reference the specific venue or project by name. Show you understand their context.
2. **Position VividWalls for their environment.** Emphasize durability, archival quality, large-format capability, and the storytelling angle (limited editions, Certificates of Authenticity as guest touchpoints).
3. **Recommend collections + multi-piece curation.** Suggest relevant collections and mention the ability to create a cohesive art program across spaces.
4. **Propose a next step.** Suggest a call, a tailored portfolio, or a pricing proposal.

### Signature

```
Best regards,
VividWalls Commercial Partnerships
vividwalls.co
```

### Example

> Dear Ms. Chen,
>
> Thank you for reaching out about art for the lobby renovation at The Langford. We've worked with boutique hotels looking to define their guest experience through original art, and I'd be glad to explore how VividWalls could complement your design vision.
>
> Our large-format canvas pieces (up to 53x72) are printed with archival inks on gallery-wrapped canvas — designed for durability in high-traffic environments while maintaining gallery-quality color depth. Each edition is limited and comes with a Certificate of Authenticity, which adds a curated, story-rich element for guests.
>
> I'd recommend reviewing our Abstract and Nature collections for lobby and corridor placements. For a property like The Langford, we can also curate a multi-piece program with cohesive color and thematic threading across spaces.
>
> Would you be open to a brief call this week to discuss the project scope? I can prepare a tailored portfolio and pricing proposal.
>
> Best regards,
> VividWalls Commercial Partnerships
> vividwalls.co

## Template 6: Supplier — Professional

**ID:** `supplier`

### Use for

Communications with Pictorem (fulfillment partner), print vendors, shipping carriers, packaging suppliers, or any operational vendor. The sender is a business partner, not a customer.

### Tone

Direct, precise, professional. No brand warmth needed — this is operational communication. Lead with order numbers, SKUs, and dates. Be clear about what you need and by when.

### Greeting

```
Hi [First Name],
```

### Structure

1. **Reference the order or issue.** Lead with the batch number, order ID, or ticket reference.
2. **State the facts.** What happened, what's expected, what's missing. Use specific SKUs, quantities, and dates.
3. **Make the ask.** One clear question or request. Don't bury it.
4. **Close efficiently.** "Thank you" is sufficient.

### Signature

```
Thank you,
Operations
VividWalls Studio
vividwalls.co
```

### Example

> Hi Marc,
>
> Following up on order batch VW-2026-0412 submitted on April 3rd.
>
> Three of the twelve pieces in this batch are showing "In Production" status but haven't moved to "Shipped" in the expected timeframe. The affected SKUs are:
>
> - VW-ABS-012 (36x48)
> - VW-GEO-008 (24x36)
> - VW-NAT-015 (53x72)
>
> Could you confirm the expected ship date for these three pieces? The customer delivery window closes April 12th and we'd like to ensure we can meet our commitment.
>
> Thank you,
> Operations
> VividWalls Studio
> vividwalls.co

## Template 7: SaaS/Platform — Technical

**ID:** `saas-platform`

### Use for

Support tickets or communications with Shopify, Stripe, Google Analytics, SendGrid, Pictorem API, or any SaaS/platform vendor. The sender is a support team or account manager at a technology provider.

### Tone

Technical, structured, diagnostic. Lead with ticket/account identifiers. Provide data points and logs. Be precise about the issue and what you need from them.

### Greeting

```
Hi [Platform] Support,
```

(Or "Hi [First Name]," if you have a named contact.)

### Structure

1. **Identifiers first.** Ticket number, store URL, account ID — whatever the platform uses to locate your account.
2. **Describe the issue with data.** What's happening, since when, frequency, and any metrics you have.
3. **State what you've verified.** Show you've done your homework — endpoint health, response times, configuration checks.
4. **Make the ask.** What do you need them to check or do?

### Signature

```
Thank you,
VividWalls Technical Operations
vividwalls.co
```

### Example

> Hi Shopify Support,
>
> Re: Ticket #SHP-2026-04-1234
> Store: vividwalls.myshopify.com
>
> We're seeing intermittent webhook delivery failures for the `orders/create` topic since April 2nd. Approximately 15% of order webhooks are returning 504 timeout errors on our endpoint.
>
> Our endpoint response time is consistently under 500ms based on our logs. We've verified our endpoint is healthy and accepting connections.
>
> Could you check if there's a delivery infrastructure issue on your end, or if our webhook subscription needs to be refreshed?
>
> Thank you,
> VividWalls Technical Operations
> vividwalls.co

## Template 8: Financial

**ID:** `financial`

### Use for

Invoice confirmations, payment scheduling, remittance communications, or financial queries with vendors, accountants, or banking contacts.

### Tone

Precise, courteous, formal. Numbers must be exact. Dates must be specific. Leave no ambiguity about amounts, terms, or deadlines.

### Greeting

```
Dear [First Name],
```

(Or "Dear [Title] [Last Name]," for formal banking/accounting contacts.)

### Structure

1. **Reference the document.** Invoice number, amount, and what it covers.
2. **Confirm or query.** State whether the figures match, or specify the discrepancy.
3. **State the action and timeline.** Payment date, method, and any conditions.
4. **Close with a window for adjustments.** Give a deadline for changes to remittance details.

### Signature

```
Best regards,
VividWalls Finance
vividwalls.co
```

### Example

> Dear Patricia,
>
> Thank you for sending invoice #PIC-2026-0398 for $2,847.50 covering the March production batch.
>
> We've reviewed the line items and they match our records. Payment will be processed via our standard net-30 terms, with the transfer scheduled for April 28th.
>
> If you need any adjustments to the remittance details, please let us know before April 25th.
>
> Best regards,
> VividWalls Finance
> vividwalls.co

## Template 9: Legal

**ID:** `legal`

### Use for

Licensing agreements, terms of service reviews, IP inquiries, cease-and-desist responses, partnership contracts, or any communication requiring legal awareness.

### Tone

Formal, measured, careful. Acknowledge receipt, confirm timeline, do not make commitments or concessions in the initial response. Keep it brief — legal replies should be precise, not expansive.

### Greeting

```
Dear [Title] [Last Name],
```

### Structure

1. **Acknowledge receipt.** Confirm you've received the document or inquiry.
2. **State the review status.** "Our team is reviewing the terms."
3. **Commit to a response date.** Give a specific date by which you'll respond with amendments or approval.
4. **Close with availability.** Keep the door open for interim questions.

### Signature

```
Regards,
VividWalls Legal
vividwalls.co
```

### Example

> Dear Mr. Rivera,
>
> Thank you for sending the updated licensing agreement for our review.
>
> We've received the document and our team is reviewing the terms. We'll provide our response with any requested amendments by end of business April 11th.
>
> If you have any questions in the meantime, please don't hesitate to reach out.
>
> Regards,
> VividWalls Legal
> vividwalls.co

## Template 10: FYI Acknowledge

**ID:** `fyi-acknowledge`

### Use for

Inbound emails that require no response — newsletters, marketing blasts, automated notifications, informational updates from vendors, or internal FYI forwards. The agent logs receipt but does not send an outbound email.

### Tone

N/A — this is an internal log entry, not a sent email.

### Structure

Log the following fields:

```
Sender:          [sender name and email]
Subject:         [original subject line]
Classification:  FYI Only
Action:          None required
Routed to:       [agent name if relevant] or N/A
```

### Example

```
Sender:          Mailchimp <noreply@mailchimp.com>
Subject:         Your March campaign report is ready
Classification:  FYI Only
Action:          None required
Routed to:       N/A
```

## Template Selection Guide

Use the table below to match inbound email signals to the correct template.

| Sender Type                | Indicators                                                                                                | Template                           |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| DTC Customer (general)     | Question about prints, sizing, materials, collections, shipping, or gifts. No issue present.              | `customer-warm` (Template 1)       |
| DTC Customer (issue)       | Complaint, damage report, wrong item, delay, refund request, or negative experience.                      | `customer-resolution` (Template 2) |
| Repeat/VIP Customer        | 3+ previous orders, trade program member writing personally, or recognized collector.                     | `customer-vip` (Template 3)        |
| Interior Designer          | Mentions "client project," "specifying," "staging," design firm name, or ASID/IIDA credentials.           | `trade-designer` (Template 4)      |
| Hotel/Restaurant/Corporate | Mentions property name, guest experience, lobby/corridor, procurement, or commercial volume.              | `trade-hospitality` (Template 5)   |
| Supplier (Pictorem)        | Order batch references, SKU numbers, production status, fulfillment timelines, or vendor account context. | `supplier` (Template 6)            |
| SaaS Platform              | Support ticket numbers, API references, webhook/integration issues, or platform account context.          | `saas-platform` (Template 7)       |
| Financial                  | Invoice numbers, payment terms, remittance details, or accounting queries.                                | `financial` (Template 8)           |
| Legal                      | Licensing agreements, contracts, IP inquiries, terms review, or legal letterhead.                         | `legal` (Template 9)               |
| Newsletter/Marketing       | Automated notifications, campaign reports, promotional blasts, or no-reply sender addresses.              | `fyi-acknowledge` (Template 10)    |

## Notes

- When an email fits multiple templates (e.g., a VIP customer with a complaint), prioritize the **relationship** template (`customer-vip`) and incorporate the **resolution** structure from Template 2.
- When in doubt, default to `customer-warm` — it is the safest starting point for any unclassified inbound email from an individual.
- All templates assume the agent has access to order history, CRM data, and the VividWalls product catalog for personalization.
- Review `voice-and-tone.md` for the full brand voice framework and `brand-guidelines.md` for visual and terminology standards.
