## Voice vs. Tone

**Voice** is VividWalls' personality — consistent across every touchpoint.
**Tone** is how that voice adapts to context — celebratory in a launch, reassuring in a support reply, authoritative in a trade pitch.

---

## 1. Brand Personality

VividWalls speaks like an art-world insider who genuinely wants you to find the right piece — knowledgeable without being pretentious, passionate without being pushy, warm without being saccharine.

### Core Traits

| Trait          | What It Means                                               | We Are                                    | We Are Not                       |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Curated**    | Every word is intentional, like every piece in a collection | Thoughtful, selective, editorial          | Verbose, cluttered, mass-market  |
| **Confident**  | We know art and we stand behind our work                    | Assured, direct, knowledgeable            | Arrogant, dismissive, hyperbolic |
| **Warm**       | We welcome everyone into the conversation about art         | Inviting, human, encouraging              | Cold, exclusive, intimidating    |
| **Expressive** | We use language that evokes feeling and space               | Vivid, sensory, evocative                 | Flowery, purple, over-the-top    |
| **Honest**     | We say what's real — limited means limited                  | Transparent, straightforward, trustworthy | Misleading, vague, sales-y       |

### Voice Spectrums

```
Formal ●――――●――○――――――――― Casual
         ↑ VividWalls sits here: polished but approachable

Simple ――――●――○――――――――――― Complex
            ↑ Accessible language, art-literate vocabulary when earned

Serious ――――――○――●――――――――― Playful
               ↑ Warm and occasionally witty, never flippant about the art

Reserved ――――――――○――●―――――― Expressive
                  ↑ Sensory and evocative, never melodramatic
```

| Corporate jargon      | "We leverage our synergistic art platform to deliver..." | Soulless; antithetical to art                            |

## 3. Tone by Context

### Marketing & Social

**Tone:** Expressive, inviting, sensory

| Channel            | Tone Notes                      | Example                                                                                               |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Instagram caption  | Lead with feeling, not features | "Neon Pulse — when your walls need a heartbeat."                                                      |
| Pinterest          | Aspirational, room-focused      | "Abstract canvas art that anchors a modern living room. Limited edition, $129."                       |
| Meta/Google Ads    | Benefit-driven, concise         | "Original wall art, limited editions, delivered with a Certificate of Authenticity. Starting at $59." |
| Email subject line | Curiosity or transformation     | "The piece your dining room has been waiting for"                                                     |
| Email body         | Warm, personal, story-driven    | "We created Ember Flow after watching a Pacific sunset in December..."                                |

### Product & Collection Pages

**Tone:** Confident, descriptive, informative

```
DO: "Ember Flow captures the last light of a winter sunset in layers of amber,
    coral, and charcoal. Printed on gallery-wrapped canvas with archival inks.
    Edition of 50."

DON'T: "Beautiful abstract painting in warm colors. Great for living rooms.
       High quality canvas print."
```

**Product description formula:**

1. Evocative opening (what it feels like)
2. Visual description (what you see)
3. Material and craft details (what it's made of)
4. Edition and authenticity note (what makes it rare)

### Customer Support

**Tone:** Helpful, calm, solution-first

| Situation        | Tone Shift                  | Example                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Shipping inquiry | Reassuring, specific        | "Your edition of Mountain Mist shipped Tuesday and should arrive by Friday. Here's your tracking link."                   |
| Damage/issue     | Empathetic, action-oriented | "We're sorry Coral Reef arrived damaged — that's not the experience we want for you. We're shipping a replacement today." |
| Return/exchange  | Understanding, no-pressure  | "If it doesn't feel right in your space, we get it. Here's how to start a return."                                        |
| Edition sold out | Honest, redirective         | "Solar Flare sold out in 48 hours. We won't reprint it — but Aurora Sky has a similar energy. Want us to hold one?"       |

### Certificates & Documentation

**Tone:** Formal, precise, authoritative

```
Certificate of Authenticity

"Cosmic Drift"
Original artwork by Kingler Bercy
Giclée print on gallery-wrapped canvas
Edition 23 of 50

This certifies that the above work is an authentic,
limited-edition VividWalls print, produced with archival-quality
materials and individually numbered.

[Artist signature]
VividWalls Studio — vividwalls.co
```

### Trade & B2B Communications

**Tone:** Professional, knowledgeable, partnership-oriented

| Audience              | Tone Adaptation              | Example                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Interior designers    | Collaborative, spec-oriented | "We can provide custom sizing for your client's feature wall. Here's our trade catalog with dimensions and lead times."                       |
| Hotel procurement     | Authoritative, ROI-aware     | "VividWalls editions are designed for hospitality environments — durable canvas, archival inks, and a brand story your guests will remember." |
| Restaurant/bar owners | Bold, atmosphere-focused     | "Fractal Double Red was made for spaces like yours — dramatic enough to hold its own against concrete and candlelight."                       |
| Corporate offices     | Clean, professional          | "Our Geometric and Minimalist collections are specified by design firms for professional environments that value visual identity."            |

### Error Messages & Functional UI

**Tone:** Calm, clear, helpful

```
DO:  "That edition is no longer available. Browse similar pieces."
DON'T: "Error 404: Product not found."

DO:  "Your payment didn't go through. Try a different card or contact your bank."
DON'T: "Transaction failed. Error code: PMT_DECLINED."

DO:  "We couldn't load that image. Refresh to try again."
DON'T: "Image load error. Please try again later."
```

## 4. Messaging Framework

### Mission

We create original, limited-edition wall art that transforms everyday spaces into personal galleries — making authentic art accessible without sacrificing quality or provenance.

### Vision

A world where every wall tells a story and original art is part of how people express who they are at home and at work.

### Value Proposition

For design-conscious people who want their spaces to feel intentional and alive, VividWalls is the art studio that creates original limited-edition prints with a Certificate of Authenticity — unlike mass-produced decor, every piece is numbered, the edition is capped, and the artist is real.

### Positioning Statement

VividWalls is the premium art studio for people who believe their walls deserve more than mass production — because original, limited-edition art should be accessible, not exclusive.

### Elevator Pitches

**10-second:**
"VividWalls creates original, limited-edition wall art — every piece comes numbered with a Certificate of Authenticity."

**30-second:**
"Most wall art is mass-produced and disposable. VividWalls is different — we create original artwork in capped editions, printed on gallery-quality materials, and every piece ships with a Certificate of Authenticity. Starting at $59, it's real art for real spaces."

**60-second:**
"Your walls say something about you, whether you style them or not. VividWalls creates original art across six collections — Abstract, Minimalist, Nature, Geometric, Portrait, and Custom — each piece limited to a fixed edition size. We print on canvas, fine art paper, metal, and acrylic with archival inks, and every order includes a Certificate of Authenticity signed by the artist. Interior designers, hotels, and restaurants use our work to define their spaces. Consumers use it to make their homes feel like them. Starting at $59, with pieces up to $349 for custom commissions."

## 6. Writing Rules

### Always

- Lead with the art, not the sale
- Use sensory language — colors, textures, light, space
- Name the specific piece when possible ("Cosmic Drift", not "this print")
- Mention the edition or authenticity when it's natural
- Keep sentences varied — mix short and medium
- Use em dashes for emphasis and pacing
- Write in active voice

### Never

- Use ALL CAPS for emphasis (italic or bold only)
- Use exclamation marks more than once per piece of copy
- Call art "product" or "item" in customer-facing copy
- Use "cheap", "affordable", or "budget" — use "accessible" or "starting at"
- Use "limited time" when you mean "limited edition" — they are different concepts
- Fake urgency ("Only 2 left!" unless it's factually true and the edition is public)
- Use superlatives without substance ("best art ever", "most beautiful prints")

### Prohibited Terms

| Avoid              | Use Instead                | Reason                       |
| ------------------ | -------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Revolutionary      | Original, distinctive      | Overused, means nothing      |
| Best-in-class      | Gallery-quality, premium   | Unsubstantiated claim        |
| Seamless           | Effortless, smooth         | Overused                     |
| Synergy            | Partnership, collaboration | Corporate jargon             |
| Leverage           | Use, build on              | Corporate jargon             |
| Disrupt            | Transform, reimagine       | Tech-bro energy, off-brand   |
| Affordable         | Accessible, starting at    | Sounds cheap                 |
| Stunning (overuse) | Striking, vivid, arresting | Reserve for true standouts   |
| Content (for art)  | Art, piece, work, edition  | Art is not "content"         |
| Drop (for launch)  | Release, launch, debut     | Streetwear energy, off-brand |

## 7. Sample Rewrites

### Product Description

**Before (generic):**

> Beautiful abstract wall art print. High quality canvas. Great for any room. Available in multiple sizes. Fast shipping.

**After (VividWalls voice):**

> Ember Flow captures the last light of a winter sunset — layers of amber, coral, and deep charcoal that shift depending on where you stand. Printed on gallery-wrapped canvas with archival giclée inks. Edition of 50, each numbered and accompanied by a Certificate of Authenticity.

### Email Subject Line

**Before:** New Art Available — Shop Now!
**After:** The piece your living room has been waiting for

### Instagram Caption

**Before:** Check out our new abstract art print! Great colors, great quality. Link in bio. #art #wallart #homedecor
**After:** Neon Pulse — electric cobalt and magenta on gallery-wrapped canvas. Edition of 50, starting at $129. The kind of piece that makes a room stop whispering and start talking. Link in bio. #VividWalls #WallArt #ContemporaryArt #AbstractArt #HomeDecor

### Error State

**Before:** Error: Item out of stock.
**After:** This edition has sold out — it won't be reprinted. Browse similar pieces from the same collection.

### Trade Outreach Email

**Before:** Hi, we sell wall art. Would you like to partner with us?
**After:** Hi [Name] — I came across your work on [Project] and loved how you used art to anchor the living spaces. VividWalls creates limited-edition prints across six collections, and I think our Geometric and Nature lines could be a strong fit for your residential projects. Happy to send our trade catalog with sizing and pricing if you're interested.
